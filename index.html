How it works

Answers

Trained only on your services, service area, hours and policies. Unknown questions go to a human.

Triages

"No heat", "burst pipe", "roof leak" → the customer is told to call your emergency line and you get an instant alert.

Books

Routine jobs are booked into Google Calendar, Cal.com or your existing booking link, with the job details attached.

Reports

Every enquiry lands in a shared sheet, and you get a plain-English summary each week.
